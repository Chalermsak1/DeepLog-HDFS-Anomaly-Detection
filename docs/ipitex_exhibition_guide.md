### Q3: *"How can you guarantee that automated self-healing won't trigger a cascading cluster failure or crash loop?"*
> **Defensible Scientific Answer:**  
> *"This is the central theoretical contribution of our USENIX paper. We do not use unconstrained heuristics or black-box reinforcement learning for actuation.*  
> *Instead, every candidate control action $u_t$ is projected through an online **Quadratic Program (QP)** constrained by Control Barrier Functions (CBF):*
> $$\min_{u} \frac{1}{2} \|u - u_{\text{nominal}}\|^2 \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \nabla h(x)^T [f(x) + g(x)u] \ge -\gamma h(x)$$
> *By Nagumo's theorem, this guarantees forward invariance of the safe operating set $\mathcal{C} = \{x : h(x) \ge 0\}$. Furthermore, we enforce a **Discrete Lyapunov Contraction condition** ($\Delta V(x) \le -\kappa V(x)$), mathematically guaranteeing that error energy decays exponentially. In 1,000 adversarial Chaos Gym tournament episodes, DeepLog-Infinity recorded **exactly zero safety barrier violations**."*

We map token sequences into 10,000-bit binary hypervectors. Classification is computed via bitwise `XOR` followed by `__builtin_popcountll` on 64-bit unsigned integers.
