Five Foundational Pillars
01

Zero-Parsing Byte Field + HDC

Raw log bytes mapped through Fourier features into 10,000-bit hypervectors. No regex parser, no out-of-vocabulary collapse.

02

Port-Hamiltonian Neural Queues

Queue dynamics constrained by energy passivity (dH/dt ≤ yᵀu), so predictions respect physical conservation.

03

Adaptive Conformal Inference

Alert thresholds with finite-sample coverage guarantees that adapt under traffic distribution shift.

04

In-Kernel eBPF XDP Actuation

Mitigation at the network-driver layer, designed for sub-microsecond packet decisions without userspace hops.

05

Provable Safety: Control Barrier Functions + Lyapunov Stability

Every automated remediation passes a QP safety filter that keeps the cluster inside its safe set and rules out oscillating, thundering-herd feedback loops.
